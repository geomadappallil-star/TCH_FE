NDIS Plan Freedom

Self-managed and plan-managed participants can choose us directly

Under the NDIS, self-managed and plan-managed participants are completely free to choose providers that aren't registered. That's how we work — a clear service agreement, honest hourly rates, and invoices your plan manager can process without you chasing anything.
